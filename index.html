Enter the customer's details or read them from a photo, check the preview, then print every line at its exact spot on the passbook page.

Printing tips
  • Choose a paper size that matches the page size shown above (or the passbook size in a passbook printer), scale 100% or "Actual size", margins none, headers and footers off.
  • Do a test on plain paper first with "Fill sample data". Lay it over a real passbook page, hold both up to the light, then correct any shift under Layout.
  • If Print does nothing here, use Save print file, open the saved file in your browser and print from there.

Page and printer

The page is the printed lower half of the open passbook, 179 mm wide. Positions were measured from a photo, so expect to nudge things by a millimetre or two after a test print.

Passbook printers use 17 characters per inch (1.49 mm) and 6 lines per inch (4.23 mm). Character width only applies to the passbook printer look.

Field positions

Pick a line, then type its position or use the arrows. Positions are in mm from the top-left corner of the page.

Background guide

Add a scan or photo of a blank passbook page, cropped to the page. It shows behind the fields to help you line things up and is never printed.

Save your layout

Your layout and branch details are remembered on this device. Customer details are never stored.
